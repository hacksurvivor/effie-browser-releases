# Effie Browser releases

Downloads and the update feed for Effie Browser, the browser you and your AI agents share.

- **Download:** the [latest release](https://github.com/hacksurvivor/effie-browser-releases/releases/latest). On a Mac, unzip `Effie-Browser-<version>.zip` and move Effie Browser to Applications.
- **Updates:** Effie Browser checks the feed on the latest release and offers new versions itself.

| System | Version | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Source and licenses

The app and its interface are closed source. Its open parts are at [hacksurvivor/effie-browser](https://github.com/hacksurvivor/effie-browser).

Effie Browser includes open source software. You can get the source of its parts under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 here:

- Fingerprinting defences ported from Brave's `brave-core`: [engine/patches](https://github.com/hacksurvivor/effie-browser/tree/main/engine/patches)
- [adblock-rust](https://github.com/brave/adblock-rust)
- [bergamot-translator](https://github.com/browsermt/bergamot-translator) and [Firefox's translation models](https://github.com/mozilla/firefox-translations-models)

From the version after 0.1.20, the app lists every license under **Effie Browser → Licenses**, or **⌘K → Licenses**.
